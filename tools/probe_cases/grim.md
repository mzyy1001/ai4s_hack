## Methods (excerpt)
Pain was scored by each patient on an 11-point integer Numeric Rating Scale (NRS, 0–10);
each patient contributed exactly one score. Ten patients (n = 10) completed the assessment.

## Results (excerpt)
The mean NRS score in the treatment group was 3.14 (SD 1.20, n = 10).
The mean NRS score in the control group was 5.70 (SD 1.40, n = 10).
The difference was significant (P = 0.002).
