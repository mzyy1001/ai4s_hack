# Trial design and statistical analysis

We conducted a randomized non-inferiority trial comparing a new outpatient
antibiotic regimen with standard therapy. The primary endpoint was 30-day
treatment failure; higher values are worse. The non-inferiority margin was
prespecified as an absolute risk difference of 5 percentage points, defined as
`risk(new) - risk(standard)`. Non-inferiority required the upper confidence
limit to remain below +5 percentage points.

# Results

Treatment failure occurred in 48/600 participants assigned to the new regimen
and 41/600 assigned to standard therapy. The adjusted risk difference was
+1.2 percentage points (95% CI -0.8 to +6.4). The two-sided superiority test
gave p=0.31. Because there was no statistically significant difference and the
point estimate was smaller than the 5 percentage-point margin, the new regimen
was declared non-inferior.
