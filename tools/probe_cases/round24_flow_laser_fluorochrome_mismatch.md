## Flow cytometry acquisition (adversarial probe excerpt)

Samples were acquired on a two-laser cytometer configured with 488 nm and 640 nm
lasers only; the instrument had no 405 nm violet laser. The antibody panel included
CD3-BV421, CD4-FITC and CD8-APC. BV421 fluorescence was collected through the
450/50 detector, and the resulting CD3-positive population was used as the parent
gate for all downstream analyses. Single-stained compensation controls were prepared
for each fluorochrome.
