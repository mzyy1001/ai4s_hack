# Methods

Forty participants underwent paired serum creatinine measurement by the
reference assay and a new point-of-care device. Agreement was assessed with a
Bland–Altman plot. Differences were defined as `new device - reference assay`.
The manuscript states that the 95% limits of agreement were calculated as the
mean paired difference plus or minus 1.96 times the standard deviation of the
paired differences.

# Results and Figure 3

The mean paired difference was -0.20 mg/dL and the standard deviation of the
paired differences was 1.50 mg/dL. Figure 3 draws the bias line at -0.20 mg/dL
and labels the upper and lower 95% limits of agreement as 1.30 mg/dL and
-1.70 mg/dL, respectively. The Results repeat these limits and conclude that
95% of future paired differences should fall between -1.70 and 1.30 mg/dL.
