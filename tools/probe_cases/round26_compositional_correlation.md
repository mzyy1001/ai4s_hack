# Microbiome methods

Stool 16S rRNA profiles were converted to relative abundances so that the 120
taxa summed to 100% within every sample. Absolute microbial load was not
measured. Pearson correlations were computed directly between the relative
abundance of every taxon pair. Benjamini-Hochberg correction was applied to the
pairwise p values.

# Results

Taxon A and taxon B showed a strong negative correlation (r=-0.88, adjusted
p<0.001). We therefore conclude that taxon A suppresses taxon B in the gut and
that their reciprocal ecological interaction explains the treatment response.
No spike-in, qPCR, flow-cytometric cell count, log-ratio analysis, or absolute
abundance sensitivity analysis was performed.
